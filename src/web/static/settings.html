HOW YOU PLAY

The choices that change the game. Also on the play screen.
MODE
Play: you steer. Balance: holds a spot. Path: drives itself.
STEER WITH
Hand: the webcam follows your palm. WASD: keys or arrows. Picked on the play screen.
HAND MOVES BY
Only when steering by hand. Tilt: angle your palm. Drag: move your palm.
MAZE
Board: the maze read from the marker drawing. Loads on the next start.
GHOST
Best: race the fastest saved lap.
BALANCE TARGET
Where balance mode holds the ball. Picked on the play screen.
EDGE GUARD Invisible walls when you steer by hand or keys.
